From this website to a "real" simulation of the coconut

  1. Geometry: FreeCAD/Onshape → bed+boulders.stl, coconut.stl (sphere D ≈ 0.175 m).
  2. Mesh: blockMesh box ~12 × 4 × 2 m + snappyHexMesh (refine to level 3–4 at the coconut and the shear line), or Gmsh/Salome.
  3. Solve: OpenFOAM interFoam (or foamRun -solver incompressibleVoF) with k-ω SST, sixDoFRigidBodyMotion with mass ≈ 2.67 kg. Expect 5–20 M cells and hours to days on a workstation for ~60 s of physical time.
  4. Quick alternative: DualSPHysics on a gaming GPU with <floatings>, dp ≈ 2 cm.
  5. Post: ParaView: the alpha.water = 0.5 iso-surface, streamlines, and the coconut's centre-of-mass track. Escape = it crosses the reattachment line.
Licence note: the prices of commercial tools vary with the contract, and many vendors offer free student editions. The point isn't that commercial tools are bad. It's that you can learn, and do serious work, without paying for them.
